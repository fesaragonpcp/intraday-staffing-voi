# Elicitation records

Anonymized records of the three telephone sessions with the store's sales manager.

| Session | Date | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 October 2026 | Initial interview (sections A–F): demand, kinds of day, persistence, service, patience, abandonment, operation, preferences |
| 2 | 2 October 2026 | Clarification (S1–S17): morning–afternoon persistence, abandonment on extreme days, definition of service time, value of a lost customer, peak hours, opportunity costs |
| 3 | 2 October 2026 | Second clarification (T, U, R, F): server occupancy, units of the customer counts, meaning of the ranges, session dates |

Files:

- `elicitation_records.csv` / `elicitation_records.xlsx`: one row per question, with the question read to the manager (Spanish, verbatim), an English summary, the numerical answers (minimum or best case, typical, maximum or worst case), and the answer as recorded in writing during the call (Spanish, verbatim). The `sessions` sheet lists date, mode, and purpose of each session.

Protocol: answers were recorded in writing in a structured spreadsheet during each call (no audio recording). Extremes were asked before typical values. In sessions 2 and 3 no figures from other sources were shown; only the manager's own earlier answers were quoted when a clarification was needed. Earlier answers were not overwritten: when the manager clarified an answer, both versions are kept (for example, A4/A5 in session 1 and S2/S3 in session 2).

Anonymization: the names of the store, the manager, and employees were removed. The manager agreed to the anonymized use of his answers.

How each answer is used in the model is given in Table S10 of the supplementary material.
